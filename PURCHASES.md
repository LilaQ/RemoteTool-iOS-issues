# RemoteTool Premium Purchases

RemoteTool offers one Premium entitlement through three choices:

- Premium Monthly — auto-renewable monthly subscription.
- Premium Yearly — auto-renewable yearly subscription.
- Premium Lifetime — one-time non-consumable purchase for current Premium functionality.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges the Apple Account and manages cancellation, billing, taxes, refunds, and Family Sharing according to the final App Store configuration. Users can restore purchases in RemoteTool Settings and manage subscriptions at [Apple Account Subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).

Free includes one Mac, 15-minute LAN sessions, complete pointer/scroll/keyboard control, Data Saver streaming, and media actions. Premium unlocks unlimited sessions and Macs, VPN/manual access, better quality, clipboard transfer, all quick actions, and Siri/Shortcuts.

Prices and availability are displayed by Apple's purchase sheet and may vary by storefront. This document does not override the terms shown by Apple at purchase time.
